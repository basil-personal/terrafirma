HOW WE CAN HELP

From a new client file
to month-end close.

01 /

Onboard a portfolio

Send the deeds, leases, division orders, and existing schedules. We use AI to extract names, tracts, and decimals, then check the entries against the documents.

THE FOCUS

An interest schedule with source references and a short list of missing records.

02 /

Work the royalty statements

We match check detail to properties and production months, compare paid decimals with the interest schedule, and flag skipped payments or unexplained changes.

THE FOCUS

A monthly reconciliation with the statement lines behind each exception.

03 /

Stay on top of open items

We maintain the lease calendar, draft operator inquiries, and track replies. You approve outreach and decide when an issue needs a landman or counsel.

THE FOCUS

One follow-up list with a responsible person, due date, and latest response.

04 /

Prepare the owner update

We assemble income by property and operator, explain changes, and draft a summary of outstanding questions. You review the packet and lead the client conversation.

THE FOCUS

A client-ready reporting draft and supporting schedules for each owner.
