A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Do I have to sell my mineral rights?

No. Management starts with understanding and organizing what you own. If you choose to explore a lease or sale, transaction support is a separate part of the conversation.

Is TerraFirma a software subscription?

TerraFirma is a managed service. You engage our team to carry out mineral management, research, royalty review, or transaction support. We use AI in that work, and agree on the scope and deliverables with you up front.

What does AI actually do?

Our team uses AI to read documents, structure information, connect records, and flag questions faster. We review the findings, investigate exceptions, and help you act on what matters.

Which regions do you work in?

Our focus is mineral-rich regions across the United States, including the Permian Basin. Record availability and service scope are assessed county by county.

What should I bring to the first conversation?

Start with the counties involved, the type of interests or transaction, and what you want to understand. You do not need to have every document organized.
